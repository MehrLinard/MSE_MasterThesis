
\section{Structure of the document}

As this document is part of the larger research initiative which started before this specific project, some of the research and solution design from the first research projects which are relevant to understanding this one have been kept in this document.

This document is intended to guide the reader through the project in meaningful steps, explaining how the project is set up and executed, and then leading into the research and conclusions. It is structured in the following way. First, the document begins with the project management chapter, which explains the methods used during the project. It provides reasoning for tool choices where applicable and lists the tools and teams with which collaboration took place.

Next, the research chapter describes the problem in more detail. It clearly defines the specific and measurable goals of the project and presents the research sources used. This allows the reader to understand the decisions made and establishes the basis for the solution design.

The subsequent solution design chapter establishes the relevant terms and definitions valid throughout the project. It documents how the problem statement is addressed and provides the architectural groundwork for the implementation. Building on the solution design is the implementation chapter, which presents the implementation of this design. It contains the work carried out by the project team and outlines any issues encountered. Next, the results chapter lists the outcomes of the project without yet evaluating them. The following discussion chapter compares the expected outcome with the achieved outcome, assessing how many goals were reached and listing their fulfilment status.

The document concludes with the reflection and outlook, summarising what went well and what did not, and offering specific suggestions on which methods to retain or replace. This will be valuable to any follow-up to the research initiative. Finally, additional materials are provided in the appendix.
