
\chapter{Topic of this research}


This chapter establishes the scientific foundation for the project using the following structure:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Use case: }The initial definition of the use case at the start of the project
    \item \textbf{Problem statement:} This defines which problem is addressed by this research
    \item \textbf{Research question: }The question to be answered forms the basis of this research
    \item \textbf{Hypotheses: }The hypotheses are built on the research question and show the more specific issues addressed in this work.
    \item \textbf{Research gap: }The research gap identifies the lack of research needed to provide a scientific answer to the research question and hypotheses.
    \item \textbf{SMART goals: }The SMART goals translate the steps to address the research gap into measurable targets for this research.
    \item \textbf{Research success definition: }What will be considered a success regarding this research.

    \item \textbf{Research methodology: }Description of the systematic approach taken in this research.
    \item \textbf{Data quality and data availability definitions: }Definitions of data quality and availability in the context of this research.
    \item \textbf{Current state of the art: }This section delves into the current state of research, providing the basis for future decisions in the project.
\end{itemize}
