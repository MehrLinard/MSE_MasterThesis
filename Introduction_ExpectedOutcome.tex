



\section{Expected outcome}

The high-level goal of this research initiative is to validate the problem statement. Additionally, in the best case, it provides a solution or steps towards a solution capable of proving the problem statement's use case. In the worst case of not being able to fulfil the problem statement, this research provides the foundation for further improvements in data quality within the defined boundaries or researches alternative ways to improve the situation within these boundaries. In any case, this work will show a structured approach to the topic, enabling the reader to understand the challenges during the project and the decisions taken over the course of its duration.
