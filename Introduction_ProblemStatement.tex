The two key problems addressed in this series of projects are, first, that data quality in this area of Swiss Post has so far been handled in an ad hoc manner, without formal definitions, frameworks, or an agreed set of KPIs; and second, that data quality is handled manually by the business at a \gls{highlevel} rather than being automated by the IT department. Both of these problems lead to issues not being detected early and to longer reaction times. As a further consequence, delays in data processing are not identified and targeted for systematic resolution, which in turn reduces management confidence in the reports. All of this prevents continuous improvement, as the checks were neither documented nor stored in a way that allowed follow-up analysis or structured refinement.
